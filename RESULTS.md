# Results

Test accuracy (%) on the public Planetoid splits. GCN and MLP: mean ± sample
standard deviation over 10 seeds. Label propagation is deterministic.
Paper values are from Kipf and Welling (2017), Table 2 (their LP row quotes
Yang et al., 2016). Generated by `scripts/benchmark.py`; per-run numbers are
in `results/accuracy.csv`.

| Method | Cora | CiteSeer | PubMed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GCN | 81.7 ± 0.6 | 70.8 ± 0.8 | 79.1 ± 0.4 |
| GCN (paper) | 81.5 | 70.3 | 79.0 |
| MLP | 55.6 ± 1.7 | 54.2 ± 1.6 | 71.3 ± 1.0 |
| Label propagation | 73.2 | 49.2 | 72.0 |
| Label propagation (paper) | 68.0 | 45.3 | 63.0 |
| Label propagation, no class mass normalisation | 71.5 | 48.6 | 21.9 |

GCN epochs before early stopping: Cora 158–200, CiteSeer 173–200, PubMed 113–173.
Test nodes with no path to any training node (label propagation cannot reach them): Cora 59, CiteSeer 310, PubMed 0.

Wall time for the whole table: 7.4 min on arm64 CPU, 2 threads, torch 2.14.1.
